# Games: what changes

- **Browse and featuring matter more.** A larger share of game installs come from Today/Games tabs, charts, and featuring. Search still matters, but don't expect the 90%+ search share some utility apps get.
- **Keyword space is crowded.** Generic terms ("puzzle", "game") are brutal. Target mechanic and mood terms ("stacking", "one tap", "relaxing", "brain teaser", "mirror puzzle") and long-tail combinations.
- **"game" in the name** is often worth it for discovery, but check the validator for repeats.
- **App preview video** carries more weight than for utilities. First seconds must show gameplay, not logos.
- **First screenshot** = the most satisfying moment of play, real gameplay.
- **Keep game screenshots 4+ (Guideline 2.3.8):** no gore, no weapon aimed at a character, even if the game is rated higher. Show the satisfying moment, not the violent one.
- **Only show what's in the build:** no concept art, unreleased levels or effects the game doesn't have (2.3.1, 2.3.3).
- **In-app events** (App Store Connect) can surface in search and browse; use them for seasonal content and updates.
- **Review prompt timing:** after a win or a personal best, never after a loss.
- **Category ranks** are a separate lever from keyword ranks; track both.
